Introduction

Hello! I am Edward Hesketh. I currently love creating embedded systems projects, and I always enjoy a good bit of programming.

I can...

  • Architect, develop, and publish software - frontend and backend - for computers.
  • Design and assemble custom PCBs using KiCAD.
  • Create 3D parts in CAD using FreeCAD, Autodesk Fusion 360, or OpenSCAD, and manufacture them using at-home or workshop tools.
  • Maintain multiple servers to host services using NixOS.
  • Automate the building of software using the Nix language.

My favourite programming languages to work with are C and Go, but I have experience in Nix, C⁠+⁠+, C⁠#, Typescript/Javascript, Python, PHP, and a little Lua.

I enjoy learning about the hidden systems of the world behind everyday things; my current favourites are graphical/UI design rules, and railway data systems. I also have a strong passion for NixOS, which I have used for multiple years on my computers and servers.

About

At the moment, I work on personal passion projects in my free time, and occasionally as part of Hack Club - an international community of teenage hackers. I am currently in full-time education at college, studying for A-Levels in Maths, Further Maths, and Physics.

The vast majority of my projects have been published under open source licences, and can be found on my GitHub, or my Radicle. I have worked on a wide range of different projects, their quality improving over time as I have improved my skills.
